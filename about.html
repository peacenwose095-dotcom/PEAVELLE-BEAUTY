Our Story

Peavelle Beauty started with a simple frustration: most "clean" beauty brands asked you to choose between effective and gentle. We didn't think that trade-off made sense, so we set out to build a line where every formula does both.

Every product is developed with dermatologists, tested on real skin tones and skin types, and never on animals. We keep our ingredient lists short and our claims honest — no miracle promises, just formulas that work, day after day.

Today Peavelle Beauty spans skincare, makeup, lip color and fragrance, but the goal hasn't changed: to help you feel like the most effortless, radiant version of yourself.
